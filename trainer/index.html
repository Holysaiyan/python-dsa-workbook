Three tricks that fix close-but-wrong answers
  1. Work from the target. Add to 31 with 17, 13, 19, 12: 17 needs 14 (not there), 19 needs 12 (there). Answer 19 + 12.
  2. Check the last digit. For 31 the last digits must make something ending in 1: 9 + 2, 7 + 4, 3 + 8. 17 + 13 ends in 0, so it cannot be right.
  3. Three numbers become two. Take the biggest, subtract it from the target, find a pair for the rest. No pair? Put it back and try the next biggest.
